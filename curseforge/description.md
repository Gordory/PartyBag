Ever finished a dungeon with three half-stacks of the same meat spread across three friends? Party Bag shares bag contents between group members, so when you hover over any item you instantly see who else has it and how many. Hand them over, merge the stacks, free up slots.

## Features

- **Party counts in the tooltip** — hover any item to see how many each party member carries.
- **Automatic sync** — bags are shared with your group automatically.
- **Lightweight** — nothing extra, only one feature.
- **Clean up after itself** — optimized to not degrade after long gaming sessions.

## Usage

Just install it — and make sure your groupmates install it too. Only players who run Party Bag share their bags.

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/pbag` | Shows whose data you have received |
| `/pbag sync` | Asks the group to resend their bags |

## Settings

Find them in **Options → AddOns → Party Bag**:

- **Show header** — the "Party bags:" title above the list (off by default).
- **Show my items** — include your own count in the list (off by default).
- **Mode** — *Short* lists only players who carry the item; *Full* lists everyone who shared their bags, with 0 for the rest.

## Notes

- Counts cover backpack and equipped bags only — bank contents are not shared.
- Built for **WoW Forever**. Works in party, raid and instance groups.
- Localized: English, Russian.
