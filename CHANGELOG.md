# Changelog

## v1.0.0

First stable release.

- See how many of an item each party member carries, right in the item tooltip.
- Bags sync automatically when you join a group, `/reload`, or your bags change.
- Settings in Options → AddOns → Party Bag: header, your own count, Short or Full list.
- English and Russian.

## v0.1.2-beta

- New settings page: show header, show my items, Short/Full mode.
- Reagent bag is now counted.
- Fixed your own items appearing as another player in the tooltip.
- Class colors no longer fade to white when the tooltip refreshes.
- Stable row order: your row first, then everyone else by name.
- `/pbag debug` for troubleshooting.

## v0.1.1-beta

- Russian translation.

## v0.1.0-beta

- First public build.
